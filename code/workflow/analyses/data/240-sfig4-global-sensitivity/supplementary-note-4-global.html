Supplementary Note 4. Global reference analysis: summary and limits

The fixed reference set comprises 77,951 genomes, with separate per-feature models for 285 metabolic-related Pfam domains and 38 non-NARP anti-defense profiles, each present in at least 30 genomes (Methods; Data 2). Coefficients describe log-odds of carriage per tenfold increase in CDS count. The feature-specific relationships provide extended context for the local functional observations. The two sets were constructed differently; their median difference does not establish a general mechanism, and this analysis is not an independent validation of the local results. Shared sequence membership was not assessed.

Table A summarizes saved Firth-penalized coefficients (Data 2 G06_firth), checked against all per-feature fits in G12_firth_feature_fits. Ecosystem and host phylum are adjusted in separate models, never jointly. These are Firth results, not ordinary logistic-regression estimates. The difference is descriptive for the fixed feature sets.

Ordinary separately adjusted models did not yield an estimable complete fixed comparison: 106 metabolic and 11 anti-defense features failed under ecosystem adjustment, and 58 and 18 under host-phylum adjustment (G05_adjusted; G11_adjusted_fits). The complete comparison, requiring every fixed feature to satisfy the fitting conditions, was estimable in 10/50 one-representative-per-cluster draws and 76/200 cluster bootstrap replicates (G07_resampling; G17_resample_contrasts; G18_resample_features). These are complete-comparison estimability counts, not positive, effect-replication, significance or detection rates. An inestimable complete comparison does not mean that every feature failed. Results among valid replicates describe only that subset; these checks did not all pass without qualification.

Why complete comparisons failed: in the one-representative-per-cluster draws every cluster is kept but the drawn representative need not carry a feature, and in the cluster bootstrap carrier clusters can be left out; a feature with zero carriers in a replicate has a constant response and cannot be fitted. In 40 of 40 failed one-representative draws and 123 of 124 failed bootstrap replicates at least one fixed feature had zero carriers; the remaining failed bootstrap replicate failed only through a fitted-probability warning (G18_resample_features). A single such warning is not taken as proof of complete separation.

Simultaneously excluding the six NAD-related domains changed the ordinary unadjusted metabolic median from 4.05 to 3.98 (G09_nad_leaveout); this was not a one-at-a-time leave-one-out analysis and does not validate all metabolic annotations.

The ecosystem and host-phylum classifications describe the same reference set and must not be added. Host phylum was Unknown for 38,815/77,951 reference genomes (G08_strata); these are reference annotation labels, not measured host ranges. Carrier counts provide the scale of support for each feature (G01_feature_fits). For 4 profiles, all carriers belong to one gene-sharing cluster (G03_profile_support; Figure 5c); a single cluster is neither a single genome nor a single host and does not establish phylogenetic independence.

Saved opportunity diagnostic

Each feature's saved fits also include an explicit opportunity diagnostic (G01_feature_fits). With N the CDS count of a reference genome, the main model is logit(p) = a + beta log10(N), and beta (slope) is the change in log-odds per tenfold increase in N. A fixed-opportunity reference model, cloglog(p) = a0 + offset(ln N), i.e. p = 1 - exp(-lambda N) with a feature-specific lambda, assumes a constant per-CDS chance of carriage; it is a simplified reference, not a measured detection model. A free-exponent model, cloglog(p) = a1 + gamma ln N, gives a dimensionless exponent gamma, with gamma = 1 under the fixed-opportunity reference; gamma is neither beta nor residual_slope. null_logit_equivalent projects the expected carrier counts of the fixed-opportunity model, on the original CDS bins, onto the same logit scale as beta; it depends on the CDS distribution and on the feature's frequency and is not a universal constant. residual_slope = beta - null_logit_equivalent is a saved descriptive difference of two slopes, not a residualised refit, a significance test or an index of selection; gamma_se is an ordinary model standard error, and cluster_se belongs to beta only.

Saved status: the fixed-opportunity model was valid for all 323 features; the free-exponent fit was accepted for 285/285 metabolic domains and 35/38 anti-defense profiles, and not for gp4.5, ocr, samase, whose gamma and gamma_se are left empty with the saved warnings and an unaccepted estimate kept for audit only. The validity, warnings and convergence of the projection fit itself were not saved, so the projection and residual_slope are reported as saved descriptive values without a validity claim. For example, aca1 has beta = 0.6312, a fixed-opportunity projection of 2.3034 and residual_slope = -1.6722, with a separately fitted gamma = 0.2740: its positive point estimate lies below this simplified reference, which is not evidence of significance, selection, suppression or lower investment. The medians of the per-feature differences are 1.7444 for the metabolic domains and -0.9735 for the anti-defense profiles. Full-precision values are in G01; no new model or test was fitted for this Note.

Category aggregation status

An earlier aggregation of anti-defense profiles into categories used a direct string join that mis-assigned profiles; its results (G14_aggregation_fits, G15_aggregation_members, the named25 rows of G05, G06, G07 and G17, and the named column of G12) are withdrawn and kept for audit only (status of each object: G19_result_status), and the former legacy contrast and the range built on it are not current results. Figure 5 uses the 38 individual profiles, which do not pass through that join. A saved post hoc repair (G20-G25) mapped profiles through census subtypes to 9 categories when the fixed 38 profiles are pooled and 10 when all observed profiles are pooled before the carrier threshold; median category slopes were 2.0865 and 2.0481, giving metabolic-minus-category differences of 1.9619 and 2.0002. These corrected definitions change the statistical units and their weights; they are post hoc comparisons of different objects, not replicates of the 38-profile estimate, and no resampling, permutation or significance result is carried over to them. All 19 corrected logit fits and fixed-opportunity fits were valid and 18 of 19 free-exponent fits were accepted; the projection was recorded invalid for all 19, so no corrected residual difference is reported.
