# Supplementary Figure 2. NARP candidates, local neighbourhoods and ORF102 structural context

## Legend (English)

(a) Membership under DefenseFinder NARP1/NARP2, K03462–K13522 co-occurrence and filtered dbAPIS NARP-targeting definitions. Column totals are shown in brackets. The five NARP2 calls are above the dashed line; vOTU0052 is outlined. C, HQ, MQ and LQ denote complete, high-, medium- and low-quality CheckV classes; percentages are estimated completeness. Definitions are not interchangeable independent validations. (b) The five NARP2 carriers and two additional nadM carriers, separated by the dashed line, across seven samples. Values are percentages of the whole breadth-masked catalogue (masked trimmed TPM/10,000), with a common logarithmic colour scale and no row standardisation. Dashes mean the existing sample-level detection criterion was not met, not biological absence. (c) Saved gene coordinates for the five NARP2 neighbourhoods, using a common bp scale and two flanking annotated ORFs where available. vOTU0131 and vOTU1643 are reversed for display; original coordinates/strand are retained. Purple arrows are NARP2 member genes; outlined arrows are nuclease-related candidates, with distinct annotation sources detailed in Supplementary Note 1. The contig end is marked; no cross-row homology or insertion event is inferred. (d) Historical sequence-domain match intervals and the selected full-length ESMFold2 model of ORF102, coloured by native pLDDT (0–1; displayed range 0.5–1), plus a local GIY-YIG comparison with I-TevI (PDB 1MK0, E75A mutant). PyMOL cealign uses 80 paired Cα atoms; the full-model domain arrangement is uncertain (cross-region PAE 24.7 Å). These are predicted fold-consistency observations, not confirmed enzymatic or anti-defense activity. ORF102 is separate from the ORF101/ORF103 NARP2 call. Former host-background panel a is retained in Supplementary Note 1, Table C and Data 1 S22; historical depth and reference-position checks remain in Note 1, Tables A and B. Coordinates, confidence arrays and alignment provenance are in Supplementary Data 4.
